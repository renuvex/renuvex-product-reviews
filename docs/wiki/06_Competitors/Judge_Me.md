# Judge.me

## Summary
Affordable, broad-feature review app. Strong on free tier. Q&A, photo/video reviews, email scheduling, multi-store group accounts.

## Strengths to learn from
- Generous free tier
- Q&A + reviews together
- Coupons-on-review
- Multi-language widget
- Detailed import/export (CSV, AliExpress import)

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Visual customization is dated relative to Loox/Okendo
- UI density can overwhelm new users

## Notes for our app
- Prioritize import/export for migration story (merchants switching platforms)
- Multi-language widget aligns with Turkish + later locales
