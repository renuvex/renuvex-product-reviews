# Judge.me

## Verification Snapshot
- Checked on 2026-06-21 through `judge.me/pricing` and the Shopify App Store listing.
- The direct Judge.me pricing page could not be read by the browser tool because it required JavaScript/bot verification.
- The official Shopify App Store listing confirms the two-plan structure: Free and Awesome at $15/month.

## Current Plans

| Plan | Verified price signal | Verified usage signal | Verified notable features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Free | Unlimited product and store reviews; unlimited visual reviews | Review widget, star rating badge, carousels, Google rich snippets/MyBusiness, trust badge/medals, Shop App/Etsy/Amazon sync, reviews importer, AliExpress sync |
| Awesome | $15/month | No order cap found in the official Shopify listing | AI replies/summaries/translations, 130+ integrations including Klaviyo/Gorgias, 16 widgets including snippets/Q&A/reviews page, advanced collection/reminders, coupons/referrals, Google Shopping, Meta/TikTok Shop sync, social push, widget/email customization and CSS |

## Product Positioning
Judge.me is the affordable broad-feature benchmark. It gives away many review fundamentals and uses the paid plan for AI, integrations, advanced collection, syndication, and customization.

## Strengths To Learn From
- Simple pricing story: one free plan and one low-price paid plan.
- Very broad feature set for the price.
- Strong migration/import story through multiple sources.
- Q&A, coupons/referrals, product grouping, multilingual/customization, and social/Google surfaces are positioned as practical merchant tools.
- 24/7 support and broad integrations are a major part of the value proposition.

## Weaknesses / Gaps For Renuvex Positioning
- Not ikas-native.
- UI density can overwhelm smaller merchants.
- Visual polish can feel less premium than Loox/Okendo.
- Its economics are difficult to copy if Renuvex includes paid video infrastructure without quota/package controls.

## Renuvex Implications
- Judge.me is the price benchmark, not the architecture benchmark.
- Renuvex should not market unlimited video without a cost model.
- Priority parity gaps versus Judge.me: import/export, review-request email, Q&A, coupons/referrals, multi-language, verified buyer, social/Google surfaces, and broader integrations.
- Renuvex can differentiate with ikas-native install, Turkish UX, ikas order-aware verified buyer, and simpler onboarding.
