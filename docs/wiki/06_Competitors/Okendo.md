# Okendo

## Verification Snapshot
- Checked on 2026-06-21 through `okendo.io/pricing` and the Shopify App Store listing.
- Official website positioning emphasizes Okendo Platform, Platform Scale, bundles, and custom pricing.
- Shopify App Store listing exposes concrete Reviews pricing: Free, Essential, Growth, and Power.
- The local snapshot was incomplete because it focused on custom/platform bundles and missed the Shopify listing tiers.

## Current Plan Surfaces

| Surface | Verified plan shape | Verified price signal | Verified usage signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Okendo official site | Platform, Platform Scale, bundles, individual products | Mostly custom pricing | Order-volume selector; Platform Scale has no usage caps and fixed annual price |
| Shopify App Store listing | Free, Essential, Growth, Power | Free, $19/month, $119/month, $299/month | Free up to 50 orders/month; Essential up to 200; Growth up to 1,500; Power up to 3,500 |

## Shopify Reviews Plans

| Plan | Price | Usage | Verified notable features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Free | Up to 50 orders/month | Automated review request emails, smart review form, rewards engine, displays, Google SEO snippets, grouping, moderation |
| Essential | $19/month | Up to 200 orders/month | Everything in Free |
| Growth | $119/month | Up to 1,500 orders/month | AI review summaries, AI review keywords, TikTok Shop integration, Q&A widget |
| Power | $299/month | Up to 3,500 orders/month | Review campaigns, advanced CSS editor, email/SMS integrations, advanced reporting, managed onboarding |

## Product Positioning
Okendo is a premium reviews, loyalty, quizzes, referrals, and surveys platform for Shopify-oriented brands. It emphasizes polished UX, profile/attribute data, bundles, and a more premium merchandising/retention stack.

## Strengths To Learn From
- Strong visual polish and premium default widgets.
- Attribute-based review data is useful for apparel and fit-sensitive products.
- AI summaries/keywords, Q&A, advanced CSS, reporting, and managed onboarding create clear tier escalation.
- Platform bundles show a path from reviews into loyalty, quizzes, referrals, and surveys without forcing all modules into the review MVP.

## Weaknesses / Gaps For Renuvex Positioning
- Not ikas-native.
- The official website pricing is custom/bundle-oriented, so cost clarity may be weaker than a simple SMB plan.
- Advanced platform breadth can be overkill for early ikas merchants.

## Renuvex Implications
- Renuvex should match the polished default UX standard, not just the feature checklist.
- Attribute/aspect questions are a good later differentiator after core review collection, verified buyer, email requests, and import/export.
- Renuvex can keep a simpler package model while still borrowing Okendo's premium visual direction.
