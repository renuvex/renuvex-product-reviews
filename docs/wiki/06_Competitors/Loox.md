# Loox

## Summary
Photo-first review app, originally Shopify-native. Strong post-purchase email flow with photo discounts, polished gallery widgets. Premium pricing.

## Strengths to learn from
- Photo-review focus (incentivize image submission with discount)
- Email automation with branded templates
- Carousel + gallery widgets that look at home in any theme
- Side-by-side review widget comparison feature in onboarding

## Weaknesses / Gaps
- Higher price tier than Judge.me / others
- Mostly a Shopify story; no native ikas integration

## Notes for our app
- Match photo-review parity early (we have image upload — need post-purchase email + photo bonus story)
- Consider built-in discount codes for review-with-photo (ikas has discount API)
