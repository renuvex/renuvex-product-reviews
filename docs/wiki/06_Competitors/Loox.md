# Loox

## Verification Snapshot
- Checked on 2026-06-21 through `loox.app/pricing`, the Shopify App Store listing, and the Loox help article for the Beginner plan.
- Current official pricing surfaces show Beginner, Convert, and Unlimited.
- The previous local `Scale` plan was not visible in the current official pricing surfaces checked in this run.
- Beginner pricing conflicts by source: the Shopify App Store listing presents Beginner as Free, while Loox Help says Beginner is $12.99/month after trial and supports 100 review request emails/month plus 500 total orders. Treat Beginner pricing as unresolved until verified inside the actual billing flow.

## Current Plans

| Plan | Verified price signal | Verified usage signal | Verified notable features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | Free on Shopify listing; $12.99/month in Loox Help after trial | 100 monthly review request emails; up to 500 total orders | Photo review discount, 10+ widgets, reviews importer, Shop App syndication, SEO |
| Convert | $49.99/month | 300 orders included, then usage-based extra order blocks | Video reviews, AI smart sorting/highlights, AI translations/replies, AI Review Stories, Google/Meta/TikTok syndication, integrations/API access, referrals, branding removal |
| Unlimited | $299.99/month | Unlimited review request emails | Unlimited referrals, unlimited upsells, priority/support-oriented enterprise positioning |

## Product Positioning
Loox is a visual social-proof app with a polished Shopify-first reviews experience. It is strong in photo/video UGC, review request automation, referrals, syndication, AI-assisted visual sorting, and polished widgets.

## Strengths To Learn From
- Clear visual-review positioning: photos and videos are central, not a hidden add-on.
- Incentive loop: photo review discounts and referral/upsell mechanics are packaged as growth features.
- Strong display surface: widgets, carousels, media gallery, star ratings, all-reviews page, and review highlights.
- Paid-tier packaging makes video reviews and branding removal clear upgrade reasons.
- API/webhooks and syndication appear in higher-value tiers, which keeps the entry tier simpler.

## Weaknesses / Gaps For Renuvex Positioning
- Shopify/global-first, not ikas-native.
- Pricing and usage model can become complex once order blocks and higher visual-review features are needed.
- Beginner price is not consistently presented across official surfaces, so public comparison copy needs caution.

## Renuvex Implications
- Renuvex should not copy Loox's full growth-suite scope immediately. The ikas-native advantage is install, storefront script handling, Turkish UX, and simpler merchant setup.
- Priority parity gaps versus Loox: review-request email, photo/video incentive settings, review carousel, import/export, and branding/package controls.
- Renuvex can differentiate with ikas order-aware verified buyer logic once implemented; Loox is not ikas-native.
